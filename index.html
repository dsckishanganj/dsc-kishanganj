Frequently Asked Questions

Common Queries Regarding Digital Signature Certificates & USB Tokens

Q1. What is a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)?

A Class 3 DSC is a high-security digital certificate used to verify identity online and sign electronic documents securely on government and corporate portals.

Q2. Is physical document submission mandatory?

No, the process is paperless! Individual applications can be completed via Aadhaar OTP and Video KYC from your smartphone.

Q3. Why do I need a USB Crypto Token?

As per regulation, digital signatures must be downloaded and stored inside FIPS-certified USB tokens (like HYP2003 or ProxKey) to prevent unauthorized copying.

Q4. Can I renew an existing Digital Signature Certificate?

Yes, expired or expiring certificates can be renewed. If you already have a working USB token, the new certificate can be downloaded into your existing token.
