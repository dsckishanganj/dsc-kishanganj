Frequently Asked Questions

Common Queries Regarding Digital Signature Certificates & USB Tokens

Q1. What is a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)?

A Class 3 DSC is the highest level of cryptographic digital signature used in India for secure digital authentication, signing online documents, e-tendering, and compliance filings.

Q2. Why do I need a USB Crypto Token?

As per CCA regulations, digital signatures must be stored securely inside a FIPS-compliant hardware USB token (like HYP2003 or ProxKey) to prevent unauthorized copying.

Q3. Can I renew my expired Digital Signature Certificate?

Yes! DSC certificates can be renewed before or after expiration. If you already have a working USB token, the new certificate can be downloaded into your existing token.

Q4. What documents are required for an Individual Class 3 DSC?

You need a copy of your PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, a recent passport-size photograph, an active mobile number, and an email address for OTP verification.
