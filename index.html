📋 Documents Required for DSC

Please keep the following documents ready for fast processing

👤 For Individual DSC

  • PAN Card Copy (Self-attested)
  • Aadhaar Card Copy (Front & Back)
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Valid Email ID & Mobile Number (for OTP)

🏢 For Organization DSC

  • Applicant PAN Card & Aadhaar Card
  • Organization Registration Proof (GST / MSME / Company Proof)
  • Authorization Letter / Board Resolution
  • Stamp & Seal of Organization
