14. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q. What is a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate?

A. Class 3 DSC sabse high-security digital certificate hota hai jo online identity verify karne aur official digital documents sign karne ke kaam aata hai.

Q. Kya USB Token zaruri hai?

A. Haan, CCA regulations ke anusar digital signature ko secure rakhne ke liye FIPS USB Token (HYP2003 / ProxKey) zaruri hota hai.
